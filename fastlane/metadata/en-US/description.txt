Anno is a Catholic daily devotional and pilgrimage companion built natively for iPhone.

Open Anno to understand what the Church is remembering today, explore sourced devotional context and sacred art, browse the liturgical calendar, or begin a pilgrimage.

Highlights:

• DAILY DEVOTIONAL
Feasts, memorials, solemnities, historical context, prayer, art, sources and confidence — presented with an atmosphere that changes with the liturgical day.

• LITURGICAL CALENDAR
Browse days and see important feasts and solemnities stand out clearly.

• FIVE FLAGSHIP PILGRIMAGES
Explore flagship routes including the Holy Land, Rome, Santiago, Guadalupe, and Anno's featured Orange County pilgrimage centered on Our Lady of La Vang.

• START CLOSE TO HOME
The Orange County exemplar connects the Our Lady of La Vang Shrine at Christ Cathedral with Vietnamese Catholic parish and community life in Garden Grove and Santa Ana.

• BUILT FOR IPHONE
Anno uses native Apple technologies for tactile feedback, maps, sharing, search and optional arrival awareness. Location is optional; pilgrimage content remains available without it.

• ENGLISH + VIETNAMESE
English is primary, with intentional Vietnamese Catholic content and localization where supported.

Anno is Catholic-first, source-aware, and designed to make both ordinary days and major sacred moments feel worth noticing.

No leaderboard. No spiritual score. No requirement to prove where you are to pray.
