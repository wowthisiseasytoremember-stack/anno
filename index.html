Anno Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026
App: Anno — This Day in Catholic History

Data Collection

Anno collects no personal data. The app:

  • Does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, location, or any other personal identifier.
  • Does not use analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or third-party tracking.
  • Does not show advertisements.
  • Does not share any data with third parties.

StoreKit

Anno uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases (future subscription content). StoreKit transactions are processed entirely by Apple and are not accessible to the app developer. Apple may collect anonymized transaction data per their privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

MapKit (Future Feature)

A future version of Anno may request location access to show nearby sacred sites and pilgrimage routes through Apple's MapKit framework. Location data is processed on-device and is not transmitted to Anno's servers. You will be prompted for permission before any location access.

User Data

Any content you create within the app (bookmarks, saved entries) is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (e.g., through the iOS Share sheet).

Children's Privacy

Anno does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date. No material change will reduce your privacy without notice.

Contact

For questions: open an issue at https://github.com/wowthisiseasytoremember-stack/anno/issues.

This app was built with the principle that sacred history should be accessible without surveillance.
