Your task is to conduct deep, authentic research into the unique cultural integration (inculturation / hội nhập văn hóa) of Catholicism in Vietnam. Your findings will be used to design and seed content for **Anno.**, a premium native iOS Catholic devotional and liturgical year calendar app. 

### Output Guidelines
- **Academic Rigor:** Use historically verified sources, standard liturgical terms, and clear distinctions between historical fact, church-approved apparitions, and local folk traditions.

Ensure Vietnamese terms use standard Catholic terminology (e.g., *Đức Mẹ* instead of generic translation for Mary; *Kinh Thánh* instead of generic Bible; *Thánh lễ* instead of general service).
- **No Hallucinations:** If a historical detail or coordinate is disputed or unrecorded, state this explicitly.

## Section 3: Liturgical Calendar, Customs & Cultural Festivals
Research how traditional Vietnamese cultural cycles are integrated with the Catholic liturgical calendar:

### 1. Lunar New Year (Tết Nguyên Đán) Liturgy
Detail the official Catholic three-day liturgy for Tết, explaining the theological focus and local customs of each day:
- **Mùng Một Tết (New Year's Day):** Thanksgiving to God (Cầu bình an cho Năm Mới / Lễ Minh Niên).
- **Mùng Hai Tết (Second Day):** Ancestral remembrance and filial piety (Kính nhớ Tổ Tiên và Cha Mẹ).
- **Mùng Ba Tết (Third Day):** Sanctification of labor and career (Thánh hóa công ăn việc làm).
- **Hái lộc Lời Chúa:** The unique tradition where parishioners pick a decorative scroll containing a Bible verse from a designated tree in the church sanctuary to guide their family throughout the year.

### 2. Tháng Hoa (Month of Flowers - May) & Dâng Hoa
- **Dâng Hoa Custom:** The elaborate floral dance rituals dedicated to Mary. Detail the choreography, regional variations, clothing (traditional *Áo Dài* with specific colors), and the symbolic meaning of different flower colors offered (Red = Martyrdom/Love, White = Purity, Yellow = Royalty/Faith, Violet = Sorrow/Penance, Blue = Hope/Mary).

### 3. Other Cultural Intersections
- **Tết Trung Thu (Mid-Autumn Festival):** How parishes celebrate this with children's processions, lantern parades, and Eucharistic themes.
- **Tháng Các Linh Hồn (All Souls' Month - November):** Graveyard prayer services (*Đọc Kinh ở nghĩa trang*) and the chanting of *Kinh Vực Sâu* (De Profundis / Out of the Depths).

  - Cultural: In Vietnam, white is traditionally the color of death, mourning, and funerals.
  - *Rules:* How should the app handle "White" in the UI to avoid triggering somber funeral associations for traditional Vietnamese users, while still respecting liturgical white?
