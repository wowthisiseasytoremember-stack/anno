# External Feedback Triage: Chronicon Sacrum Spec
Updated: 2026-07-03

## Summary

An external agent proposed a "Chronicon Sacrum" spec with heavy gamification, ads, consumables, streak protection, daily rewards, XP, badges, and aggressive monetization.

Decision:

- Keep the useful retention and screen-architecture ideas.
- Reject trust-destroying mechanics.
- Keep **Anno** as the working brand.
- Make monetization sharper through archive, art, audio, source depth, Vietnamese, and Pilgrim utility, not through ad-tech, guilt, or fake religious economies.

The useful target is:

> Evil -20%, AI slop -100%, facts +20%, monetization +20%.

## Competitor Privacy Reality

Do not claim that Hallow is "literally stealing data" unless counsel and evidence support that exact statement.

The better wedge:

- Hallow's own privacy policy says it does not share "Private Sensitive Personal Data" with advertising partners.
- Mozilla's Privacy Not Included review says Hallow uses personal information for advertising and shares some data with marketing/advertising partners.
- Anno can compete by being subscription-first, source-first, and ad-tech-minimal.

Positioning:

> No ad-tech devotional surveillance. No AI slop. Sources on every claim.

## Accepted

### Art-first Today screen

Use sacred art earlier and larger.

Accepted:

- Hero artwork card above most long text.
- Artwork provenance visible.
- Art lightbox as a Premium conversion path.

### Liturgical color awareness

Use Catholic seasonal color as restrained accent state:

- Advent/Lent purple.
- Christmas/Easter white/gold.
- Ordinary Time green.
- Martyr red.
- Marian blue.

### Gentle progress surface

Use progress as memory, not pressure.

Accepted:

- "Days Discovered."
- Reading history calendar.
- Milestones at 3, 7, 30, 100, 365.
- Collection achievements based on real product depth.

Copy style:

- Good: "7 days discovered."
- Bad: "Don't break the chain."

### Collection achievements

Allowed examples:

- Rome Reader: read 5 Rome-linked entries.
- Art Pilgrim: open 25 art sheets.
- Source Reader: open 25 source sheets.
- Marian Month: read 30 Marian entries.
- First Route: save a pilgrimage route.

These are product-depth cues, not ecclesial status.

### Screenshot categories

Keep:

- Today.
- Sacred Art.
- Pilgrimage Map.
- Calendar Archive.
- Source Confidence.
- Your Sacred History: saved entries, routes, reading history.

## Modified

### Streaks

External proposal:

- Streak counter, "Don't break it", late-night warnings, paid restore.

Anno decision:

- Use "Daily Rhythm" / "Days Discovered."
- No paid restore.
- No shame copy.
- No "Grace Tokens."
- No push-notification anxiety.

### XP and levels

External proposal:

- Catechumen, Monk, Priest, Bishop, Doctor ladder.

Anno decision:

- Reject clerical/theological ranks as user levels.
- Use collections and milestones instead.

### Daily rewards

External proposal:

- Random blessing chest, countdowns, auto-claim perks.

Anno decision:

- Reject lottery mechanics.
- Use curated seasonal samples instead.

Allowed:

- A free Lourdes route preview on Our Lady of Lourdes.
- A free Marian audio sample during May.
- One weekly premium preview surfaced editorially.

## Rejected

- Banner ads.
- Interstitial ads.
- Rewarded ads.
- "Grace Tokens."
- Paid streak repair.
- "Indulgence" purchase badge.
- XP multipliers.
- Clerical rank levels.
- Leaderboards.
- Parish/community mode for v1.
- Tip jar as a primary monetization surface.
- Low-res-only sacred art as an irritation tactic.
- Hiding basic source/confidence behind Premium.

Reason:

These may increase short-term extraction, but they corrode the exact trust Anno needs to monetize at a premium price.

## More Aggressive, Still Tasteful Monetization

Use these instead:

- Premium archive gate after clear curiosity: past/future date, saint search, related feast.
- Premium art gallery after the user opens multiple art sheets.
- Premium audio after a limited weekly sample.
- Premium expanded source sheets for users who open sources repeatedly.
- Premium Vietnamese reviewed content pack once quality is real.
- Pilgrim route packs for concrete travel utility.
- Seasonal passes only if they are editorially excellent: Lent, Advent, Marian May, Rome Holy Week.

Strong copy:

- "Unlock the archive."
- "Open every sacred place."
- "Listen to the daily entry."
- "Save this route for Rome."
- "Read the full source notes."

Avoid:

- "God is calling you to subscribe."
- "Protect your grace."
- "Do not break the chain."

## Product Bible Updates

Keep the existing Anno docs as authority:

- Catholic-first.
- Native SwiftUI.
- Vietnamese-ready.
- Free current-day entry.
- Premium archive/map/art/audio/source depth.
- Pilgrim route packs.
- No ads in v1.
- No source-free claims.
