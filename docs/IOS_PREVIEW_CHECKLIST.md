Updated: 2026-07-03

- Today EN at iPhone SE width.

## Required Checks

- Today is the first usable screen, not a landing page.
- Vietnamese title, hero line, summary, prayer prompt, and confidence note wrap without clipping.
- July 5 appears as Fourteenth Sunday in Ordinary Time.
- Week map has six pins/list rows because July 5 has no fixed place.
- Source transparency is visible without a premium gate.
- Traditional/contextual confidence is visually distinct from confirmed confidence.
- Paywall copy sells archive, art, maps, audio, saved collections, and sources without guilt or spiritual-pressure language.
