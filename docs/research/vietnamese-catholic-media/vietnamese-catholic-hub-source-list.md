# Vietnamese Catholic Media Hub — Source List
## Aggregator/linkboard dataset for launch in Westminster, CA (Little Saigon)
**Research date:** August 18–19, 2026 (Pacific). Companion to `vietnamese-catholic-media-directory.md` (same session).
**Users:** Vietnamese-first speakers in Westminster/Orange County consolidating scattered Catholic media into one hub.

**Category tags used below**
- **[A]** Mainstream Catholic content **dubbed/subtitled in Vietnamese** (e.g., papal/Vatican content with Vietnamese dubbing/subtitles)
- **[B1]** **Vietnamese-produced** Catholic content (Vietnamese parishes, clergy, organizations)
- **[B2]** Vietnamese-language Catholic content produced by **international Catholic organizations' Vietnamese services** (equally valid secondary content per spec)
- **[C]** **Localization candidate** — significant free/openly-licensed Catholic content *not* in Vietnamese that the hub could link, host, or subtitle/dub (licensing notes included)

**Status legend**
- ✅ **VERIFIED LIVE** — content page/stream/channel fetched or confirmed posting current content on 2026-08-18/19 (Group 1)
- 🟡 **LISTED / UNCONFIRMED TODAY** — exists and confirmed via official or recent (2025–mid-2026) listings, but the stream itself was not re-fetched today; links presumed good (Group 2)
- 🔴 **OFFLINE / BROKEN** — found but failing now (Group 3) · ⚪ **HISTORICAL / ARCHIVE** — no longer airing but accessible (Group 3)

**Download/format legend** — how each source can be consumed by the hub: *embed* (iframe/official player), *RSS* (podcast feed), *stream-only* (no official download), *download available* (direct file), *app* (mobile app), *broadcast* (radio frequency).

# LOCALIZATION CANDIDATES — [C] free/important non-Vietnamese Catholic content for the hub to host/link/subtitle

> Framing: the hub can legally **link and embed** almost everything above. For **re-hosting files or adding your own Vietnamese subtitles/dubs**, the licensing below is what was found in official terms on 2026-08-18.

1. **Vatican News / Vatican Media (papal broadcasts, Pope Leo XIV events, daily content)**
   - **License found (official Legal Notes, vatican.va & vaticannews.va):** content owned by the **Dicastery for Communication (DPC)**; free to access for **personal, non-profit use**; *"reproduction and collection of Contents for reasons other than personal use is expressly prohibited in the absence of prior authorization issued in writing"*; sharing via the provided social embeds is allowed; **no commercial exploitation**.
   - **What this means for the hub:** embed official YouTube players and link freely (including the already-Vietnamese Vatican News VI output — no localization needed); for **downloading/mirroring papal video or adding custom Vietnamese subtitles to Vatican Media footage, request written authorization** from the DPC (policies@spc.va; production-rights pathway via comunicazione.va, which licenses material per-format, non-perpetually).
