## MON 2026-11-23 — St Clement I (+ Columban, Bl Miguel Pro)

**Verified facts**
- Clement: first successor of Peter about whom anything definite is known; early lists 3rd/4th pope; Irenaeus says he heard the Apostles preach. Genuine work: Letter to the Corinthians (c. end of 1st c.), written to heal a schism. Martyrdom (exile to Crimea, anchor, thrown into sea) = **later legend, traditional confidence**. Relics brought from Crimea by St Cyril (c. 868), enshrined by Adrian II in Basilica di San Clemente, Rome. Source: https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04012c.htm
- USCCB page confirms all three optional memorials on this date: https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/112326.cfm
- Miguel Pro: Jesuit, executed by firing squad 1927-11-23, Mexico City; died arms outstretched crying "¡Viva Cristo Rey!"; execution photo exists and is real. Source: https://www.catholicworldreport.com/2024/11/23/what-the-executioner-saw-the-death-of-blessed-miguel-pro/
- Columban (543–615): Irish missionary monk, founder of Luxeuil and Bobbio (background from CatholicCulture day-page snippet; not deep-treated in entry).

**[VI]** Pro's "Viva Cristo Rey!" — Vietnamese equivalent cry of the martyrs: "Đạo ta vạn thắng" is *not* standard; better to render the Spanish + gloss. Pro died one day after Christ the King — thematic bridge to 11/22 (outside batch).
**Terminology:** Giáo hoàng Clêmentê I · Chân phước Micae Pro · linh mục Dòng Tên (Jesuit).

## TUE 2026-11-24 — ★ CÁC THÁNH TỬ ĐẠO VIỆT NAM (flagship)

**Verified facts**
- Memorial, red. USCCB: https://bible.usccb.org/bible/readings/112426.cfm (Gospel Lk 21:5-11, "not a stone upon a stone").
- Persecutions: 50+ anti-Christian edicts between 1625 and 1886; estimates 130,000–300,000 Catholics killed; worst under **Minh Mạng, Thiệu Trị, Tự Đức**; crucifix-trampling edicts; "tả đạo" branding. Sources: https://www.cbcvietnam.org/martyrs-under-nguyen-dynasty-persecution-faith/ (CBCV), https://www.causesanti.va/it/santi-e-beati/martiri-del-vietnam.html (Dicastery for Causes of Saints).
- 117 canonized by John Paul II **1988-06-19** (note: June, not July — EWTN snippet says July 19; CBCV/CNA-derived sources say June 19; the June 19, 1988 date is the commonly cited one — flag for Agent C). Beatified in 5 groups: 1900 (Leo XIII, 64), 1906 & 1909 (Pius X, 8 + 20), 1951 (Pius XII, 25). Source: causesanti.va.
- Composition: 96 Vietnamese, 11 Spanish, 10 French; 8 bishops, 50 priests, ~59 lay.
- **Anrê Trần Dũng Lạc:** b. c.1795 Bắc Ninh, family moved to Hanoi, baptized by catechist, ordained 1823, twice arrested/ransomed, beheaded **1839-11-24** Hanoi (Ô Cầu Giấy) under Minh Mạng. Sources: EWTN https://www.ewtnnews.com/world/asia-pacific/st-andrew-dung-lac-and-companions-vanguards-of-the-faith-in-a-time-of-persecution ; catholicreadings.org.
- **Têôphan Vénard:** MEP priest, b. 1829 Poitou; clandestine ministry in W. Tonkin ~6 years; betrayed, caged; letter to his father: "A slight sabre-cut will separate my head from my body, like the spring flower which the Master of the garden gathers for His pleasure…" — text verified in Catholic Encyclopedia vol. 14 (CCEL scan: https://ccel.org/ccel/h/herbermann/cathen14/cache/cathen14.pdf). Beheaded 1861-02-02 near Hanoi under Tự Đức. Beloved by St Thérèse of Lisieux.
- **Anê Lê Thị Thành:** b. 1781, mother & grandmother, Ninh Bình; arrested for sheltering priests; long interrogations/torture; died in prison 1841; the only laywoman among the 117; relics at Phúc Nhạc church (Phát Diệm area). Sources: Roman Martyrology 2004 entry (boston-catholic-journal PDF), laudes.org pilgrimage PDF, stagneslethithanh.org.
- **Anrê Phú Yên (Blessed, not among the 117):** b. 1625, catechist of Alexandre de Rhodes; arrested at de Rhodes' house; hanged + beheaded **1644-07-26/27** (sources differ by a day — flag); beatified 2000-03-05 by JPII; venerated as **protomartyr of Vietnam** (traditional title). Source: New Catholic Encyclopedia Jubilee vol. entry ("ANDREW THE CATECHIST, BL."), thevietnamese.org.
