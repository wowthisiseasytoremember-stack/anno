## The short version

The spec is strong. The chapter model, the three-layer arrival, the ARRIVE/SEE/KNOW/PRAY/CONTINUE grammar, the refusal to make GPS "DRM for prayer," and the "not yet" list are all correct and unusually disciplined. Decision B is right. Decision A is right.

The three real problems:

1. **The arc promises migration; no station delivers it.** Mary → persecution → witness → *migration* → community → next generation. Chapters I–IV cover everything except the hinge. Right now the ocean is crossed in a tagline and never on the ground.
2. **Chapters II, III, and IV are the same chapter three times.** Three suburban parishes in a row, each with a "living community" theme. Without a distinct physical thing to *see* at each, users will experience it as "another Vietnamese church" — the exact failure mode you name in §6.
3. **The route and Station 3 share a name.** "Our Lady of La Vang — Orange County" contains a stop called "Our Lady of La Vang Church," and Station 1A is also "Our Lady of La Vang." Three things with one name inside one product is a navigation bug disguised as a title.

Everything below works outward from those three.

## 1. Fix the arc: add the Threshold

The historical fact your route is built on: Orange County became the largest Vietnamese Catholic community outside Vietnam because **Camp Pendleton** was the first refugee reception center in April 1975, and people settled near where they landed **[verify numbers/dates]**. The diocese later produced the first Vietnamese-born U.S. bishop (Dominic Mai Thanh Lương, auxiliary of Orange from 2003, d. 2017) and its current Vietnamese auxiliary, Bishop Thanh Thái Nguyễn, who I believe himself fled by boat **[verify]**. That's a complete story and the app currently skips the middle.

Options, in order of my preference:

- **A narrated, non-driven "remote station."** Camp Pendleton is too far and access-restricted — so make it the first station you *don't visit*. Open it from the car before leaving Christ Cathedral: "Forty miles south of here, in 1975, tents…" This also proves your §18 principle in the product itself: sacred content works without physical presence.
- **A cemetery station.** Good Shepherd Cemetery in Huntington Beach is the diocesan Catholic cemetery, and Westminster Memorial Park holds a Vietnamese Boat People Memorial (2009) **[verify both]**. Vietnamese Catholics celebrate Lễ Các Đẳng (All Souls) at cemeteries with real intensity. A quiet "the first generation rests here" station is the most honest migration stop available, and it gives the route a tonal register no parish can.
- **The Vietnamese Catholic Center (Trung Tâm Công Giáo Việt Nam), Santa Ana** **[verify address/role]** — the institutional heart of the community since the 1980s, where the big Martyrs feast and Tết celebrations happen. Less emotional, more "this is how the community organized itself."

Recommended arc, revised:

**Mary → persecution/witness → crossing → the first generation → parish life → handed forward**

## 2. Differentiate the parish chapters — or cut one

Three parish stops only work if each has a distinct *SEE* that cannot be substituted. Research question for each: what is physically there? Most Vietnamese parishes have a La Vang grotto or statue outdoors **[verify at each]** — and that gives you a throughline that is better than anything in the current spec:

> **"Find Mother."** At every stop after the Shrine, the SEE prompt is: find the image of Đức Mẹ La Vang here.

Once at the monumental shrine, then small, in a corner by the parking lot, then in a parish hall. The user discovers for themselves that La Vang left the monument and went everywhere. That *is* Chapter III's thesis ("La Vang lives here too"), but shown rather than told, and it makes the parishes feel like one unfolding idea rather than a list.

## 5. Vietnamese: specific copy notes

Have native Vietnamese Catholics review, as you said — but a few things to bring to that review:

- **"Trao lại" vs. "truyền lại."** *Trao lại* leans toward "hand back/return." For "handed forward," *Đức tin được truyền lại* (handed down) is the natural phrase. Flag this.
- **"Faith crossed an ocean" → "Đức tin đã vượt biển."** *Vượt biển / vượt biên* is literally the boat-people word. In Vietnamese this line stops being Hallmark and becomes almost unbearably direct. That may be exactly right — but it's a decision the community makes, not the design team. Be aware the two languages' emotional loads will be unequal, which is itself an argument for your "two authored faces" principle.
- **There is no neutral "you" in Vietnamese.** "YOU'VE ARRIVED" → *Bạn đã đến* uses a peer pronoun that's wrong for an elder. Options: drop second person entirely (*Đã đến nơi*), use formal *quý vị*, or let prayers use the standard *chúng con* ("we, your children") which is how Vietnamese Catholics actually pray. Design the register once; it affects every string.
- **"Mẹ."** Vietnamese Catholics address Mary as *Mẹ* (Mother) — "Lạy Mẹ La Vang." Station 3's title might land harder as *Mẹ La Vang vẫn ở đây* than the current phrasing. Ask.
- **Use the real prayers.** The PRAY layer should carry the canonical Vietnamese *Kinh Kính Mừng*, not a translation of the English Hail Mary. Vietnamese Catholics also have a widely known La Vang hymn and prayer **[identify and license]**.
- **Typography is a real risk.** Hero-size uppercase Vietnamese with stacked diacritics (Ộ, Ễ, Ắ) breaks line-height and clips in many display fonts. Specify a face with full Vietnamese support and test the opening screen in Vietnamese *first*.

## 6. Color caution

Martyrs station = crimson + gold. Liturgically correct. But bright red + yellow is also the color pair of the flag of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, in a community where the yellow-with-three-red-stripes heritage flag is everywhere. Use deep liturgical red with muted gilt, not saturated red with bright yellow. Put this in front of community reviewers explicitly; don't let them discover it.

## 7. Who holds the phone

You list elderly relatives as a core user but the spec is visual-first. The real test: a 24-year-old hands the phone to her grandmother in the Marian Garden. Grandmother will not read a paragraph on a phone. She *will* listen.

- **Audio-first PRAY layer in Vietnamese**, with a real voice, not TTS.
- **A "Bà/Ông mode"**: large type, audio autoplay, no gestures beyond tap.
- Longer term: the "Go deeper" layer is the natural home for recorded first-generation testimony — thirty seconds of someone who was at Camp Pendleton. That's premium content people would pay for and parishes would help you gather.

## 8. Etiquette and liturgy, not schedules

Agree completely: no baked-in Mass times. But *do* build **etiquette** into the station: Mass may be in progress; silence the phone; don't photograph during liturgy; the Blessed Sacrament chapel is for prayer. Add a one-tap **Quiet Mode** that suppresses haptics and bloom for users already inside a church. "If every station explodes none of them matter" also applies to a phone buzzing during the consecration.

Then use the calendar for retention: **August 15** (Assumption/La Vang), **November 24** (Vietnamese Martyrs), **Tết**, **All Souls**. These are the four days a completed pilgrim has a reason to reopen Anno. Build the "pilgrimage day" notification before building any social feature.

## Decisions A–H

**A — One chapter, not three stops.** Yes. Non-negotiable.

**B — Add Our Lady of La Vang Church.** Yes, with the station renamed "The Parish" and its founding story as the KNOW card.

**C — St. Barbara.** Provisional. Keep only if site research shows the strongest school/youth/sacramental evidence of any OC Vietnamese parish; otherwise swap, or cut to two parishes and give the slot to the Threshold station.

**D — Variants.** Three tiers is right; four is one too many. "Group / Parish Day" is a *mode* (pauses for Mass, meal), not a route — make it a toggle on Full. Keep Quick / Half Day / Full and add nothing until driven.

**E — Tone.** English line is fine; slightly soft, not Hallmark. The Vietnamese line will be far heavier than the English — decide whether that asymmetry is intended, with the community.

**F — Spectacle.** Go harder on the *Shrine* and *completion*; go quieter on everything else. The spectacle budget is relative. The biggest upgrade isn't more bloom; it's a longer, slower reveal and the hinge sentence into the Martyrs.

**G — Framing.** Your vote is right: a Vietnamese Catholic pilgrimage that welcomes everyone. The other framing produces a diocesan brochure. Just ensure the English KNOW cards give a non-Vietnamese Catholic enough to feel invited rather than observing.

**H — Arc.** Nearly right. Replace with: **Mary → persecution and witness → crossing → the first generation → parish life → handed forward.** Then build a station for the crossing, because right now the most important word in the arc is the only one with nowhere to stand.
