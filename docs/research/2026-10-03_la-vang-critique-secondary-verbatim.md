### Key Critiques and Improvements
**1. Route composition and stops (A, B, C)**  
Christ Cathedral complex as **one chapter with substations** is correct and cleaner. The shrine (12-ft Carrara marble Mary in áo dài + khăn đống under banyan/rainforest canopy), Martyrs Wall (117 names with death details, steel, linked to the Alpha ribbon), and Marian Gardens (four Rosary mystery areas with Peruvian sacred art, serene niches) form a natural progression: invocation → witness → quiet prayer. Optional Cathedral interior for continuity is fine but secondary.

**Strongly include Our Lady of La Vang Church (Santa Ana, 288 S Harbor Blvd)**. Omitting a living parish that carries the exact name would feel incomplete for a Vietnamese-Catholic OC exemplar. Make it intimate and parish-centered (not competing with the monumental shrine). Theme it as devotion embedded in everyday community life.

**Saint Columban (Garden Grove)** is excellent for “living parish / faith becomes community.” It has deep Vietnamese roots, multiple Viet Masses, school, multicultural character, and historical ties (Msgr. Tuan Pham helped design the shrine statue). Keep the deliberate contrast: less monument, more “this is where people keep doing this every week.”

**Saint Barbara (Santa Ana)** works for generational/diaspora continuity (strong Viet presence, school, history of welcoming 1975 refugees, multi-gen ministries). It is replaceable if site research or community input reveals a stronger tell (e.g., Vietnamese Catholic Center for cultural/formation emphasis, or another parish with particularly vivid multi-gen stories). Prioritize narrative function and drive logistics over locking the manifest. All stops are geographically tight in the Garden Grove / Santa Ana / Little Saigon corridor (roughly 1–4 miles between key points), supporting half-day/full variants.

**Recommended hierarchy (refined)**:  
- Chapter I — La Vang (Christ Cathedral campus): 1A Shrine (Major), 1B Martyrs Wall (Highlight), 1C Marian Gardens (Reflection), optional Cathedral.  
- Chapter II — Living Parish: St. Columban (Highlight: Faith Becomes Community).  
- Chapter III — La Vang Lives Here: Our Lady of La Vang Church (Major: intimate continuity).  
- Chapter IV — Handed Forward: St. Barbara or best alternative (Highlight: generations, sacraments, belonging).  

Add soft “optional pause” suggestions (nearby Vietnamese eateries, cultural spots) without turning it into tourism.

**2. Variants (D)**  
Quick / Half Day / Full is useful but risks product-y overcomplication for v1. Soften labels further and lead with intent:  
- Visit La Vang (campus chapter only) — first-timers, elderly, casual.  
- La Vang + One Parish — short local.  
- Full Orange County Pilgrimage.  
- Group/Parish Day (add Mass/meal/prayer pause prompts).  

Do not surface exact minutes until driven and tested multiple times (traffic, parking, dwell time, accessibility). App can show estimated ranges post-testing. Default to Full as the exemplar experience.

**3. Opening, tone, and emotional arc (E, H)**  
“Faith crossed an ocean. Its story continues here.” is good but slightly Hallmark. Make it more specific and rooted:  
- “In 1798 she appeared in the rainforest of La Vang. Her children carried that hope across an ocean. The story continues here.”  
Or bilingual pair with equal weight.  

Arc is strong: Mary (refuge/hope) → persecution/martyrs → migration/diaspora → memory → living community → next generation. Tighten the chain with concrete OC markers: 1798 La Vang → Vietnamese martyrs → 1975 Fall of Saigon / boat people / Camp Pendleton processing → Little Saigon flowering → Christ Cathedral shrine as thanksgiving → parish life and Marian Days today. Avoid reducing diaspora to “suffered then succeeded.” Frame as faith that survived and became generative.

Add multi-gen prompts: “If you are with elders, ask them…” or “If with children, point out…” Completion can invite “What will you hand forward?”

**4. Spectacle and ritual (F)**  
Differentiate hard, but keep sacred dignity. Shrine arrival can go “ceremonial maximum” (Marian blue/gold bloom, strong haptic, larger type, optional short invocation, slower reveal) because the physical statue and canopy are already spectacular. Martyrs: slower, crimson/gold, individual name resolution, “Choose one name. Pause there.” Gardens: radical quiet (no banner, soft prompt for a decade of the Rosary or silent time).  

Completion: gold fill + illuminating stations + “PILGRIMAGE COMPLETE” + prayer + stats is excellent. Ensure it feels earned, not gamified. Share artifact: illuminated A + Marian blue/gilt + route line + date + invocation + station count — intentionally sentimental keepsake.  

Ritual grammar is gold. Enforce consistency so it becomes muscle memory by station 2–3. Add optional audio (short Vietnamese/English prayer or soft traditional element) only if it enhances presence, not distracts.

**5. Framing and identity (G)**  
**Primary frame: Vietnamese Catholic pilgrimage that warmly welcomes everyone.** This matches the shrine’s purpose, Marian Days scale (15k–20k+), and community reality (largest Vietnamese population outside Vietnam; significant share of OC Catholics and clergy). Secondary: local Catholics, families, curious visitors, youth reconnecting with heritage. Do not dilute into generic “OC Catholic.” Lead with the specific story; the universality emerges from it.

**6. Content, practical, and bilingual**

**7. Location behavior and accessibility**  
Manual “I’m here” + maps handoff is correct for v1. Optional proximity later. Explicitly support home revisit, elderly/limited mobility (campus walking distances, parking), indoor accuracy issues, privacy. Large Cathedral campus needs clear wayfinding notes inside the app for substations.
