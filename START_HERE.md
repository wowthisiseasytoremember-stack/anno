## Canonical v1 decisions

- Native SwiftUI, iOS-first.
- Today + Calendar + Map are the active core surfaces.
- Orange County / Our Lady of La Vang is the **featured, physically testable pilgrimage exemplar**.
- St. Barbara remains a core stop.
- Five flagship route packs remain available; the SoCal exemplar is featured.
- Free + Premium **$49.99/year** is the current monetization direction. Do not resurrect old monthly/multi-tier plans by default.
- English-first; Vietnamese receives authored treatment where present, and SoCal exemplar Vietnamese copy requires human Vietnamese Catholic review before being called final.
- AR, Watch, Dynamic Island, Vision, NFC, beacon, Image Playground/Genmoji and visionOS work are **Apple-native expansion lanes**, not reasons to destabilize the iPhone v1.
- No mandatory GPS. Manual **I'm Here** always works.
- No XP, leaderboards, or gamified martyrdom.
- Do not embed volatile Mass/confession/tour schedules into durable devotional prose.
- Do not treat archived docs, old 18-route/72-sanctuary catalogs, or old SwiftData plans as current product authority.
