The Solemnity of the Assumption celebrates the dogmatic truth that Mary, the Mother of Jesus, was assumed body and soul into heavenly glory at the end of her earthly life.
The dogma of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary was solemnly defined by Pope Pius XII in his 1950 apostolic constitution Munificentissimus Deus, declaring that the Immaculate Mother of God, having completed the course of her earthly life, was assumed body and soul into heavenly glory. This feast represents the ancient, unbroken tradition of both the Eastern and Western Churches, celebrating Mary's unique participation in the Resurrection of her Son.
