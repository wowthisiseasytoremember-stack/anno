# Vietnamese Catholic Streaming & Broadcast Media Directory
## For outreach to Vietnamese Catholic audiences in Westminster, CA (Little Saigon) & Orange County

**Prepared:** August 18, 2026 (all times Pacific unless noted)
**Purpose:** Reference directory of active, digitally accessible Vietnamese-language Catholic streaming/broadcast media for a marketing or outreach team.
**Scope:** (1) Diocese of Orange / Westminster-area sources first; (2) Vietnamese Catholic media across the U.S.; (3) global Vietnamese Catholic media accessible online from Westminster (Vatican, Radio Veritas Asia, Vietnam-based Church media, diaspora). Catholic-only per request; non-Catholic religious media excluded.

---

## 0. How to use this directory

**Verification tiers (date of check: 2026-08-18):**
- ✅ **Verified active** — live content posted within the last few days/weeks confirmed directly on the source's own page (fetched today) or on an official current schedule (2026).
- 🟡 **Verified via official/recent listing** — confirmed by a diocesan, federation, or parish listing dated 2025–mid-2026; schedule details may have shifted — reconfirm before citing air times in outreach copy.
- 🔴 **Found but currently inaccessible** — the source exists but its site/stream failed verification today (details noted).
- ⚪ **Unverified / historical** — documented archive or legacy page; treat as background, not as a live channel.

**On "political orientation":** This field is reported honestly rather than mechanically. The overwhelming majority of Vietnamese Catholic media is **liturgical/devotional content with no identifiable political orientation** (Masses, rosaries, catechesis, Bible reflections). Where a real editorial orientation exists, it is described and flagged below (see §9 and per-entry "Orientation" lines). No outlet in this niche identifies as "left/liberal," and we have not invented such labels; within each category below, entries are instead ordered by **(a) relevance to Westminster/Orange County, then (b) verification strength**, with orientation flags clearly marked so the team can weigh them.

**Time-zone note for live content:** Vietnam is UTC+7 = **+14 hours from Pacific**. A 6:00 PM Sunday Mass in Saigon airs at **4:00 AM PDT / 5:00 AM PST** in Westminster — use the archived replays (posted within hours on each YouTube channel) for anything targeted at U.S. viewers.

## 8. INTERNATIONAL / OTHER

### 8.1 VietCatholic's footprint (U.S./global) — orientation detail
VietCatholic News + Công Giáo TV (§2.1, §7.1) is the largest overseas Vietnamese Catholic media brand: web since 1996, TV since 2007/2011, ~724K-subscriber YouTube, 24/7 stream, SoCal studios (LA + OC). **Orientation flag for outreach planning:** its news side carries a hard anti-communist/anti-authoritarian editorial line (anti-Hanoi, anti-CCP, anti-Kremlin coverage; harshly opinionated headlines). ### 8.4 ⚑ Flagged state-affiliated outlet (include with clear distinction)
- **Báo Người Công giáo Việt Nam** — published by the **Ủy ban Đoàn kết Công giáo Việt Nam** (Committee for Solidarity of Vietnamese Catholics), a state-sponsored body under the Vietnamese Fatherland Front, **not** an organ of the Catholic Church. Access: ubdkcgvn.org.vn. 🟡 site live. **Orientation: state-affiliated/"patriotic Catholic" line — flag as such; do not present as Church media.** Useful only to monitor how state-adjacent media covers the Church; almost certainly wrong vehicle for diaspora outreach.

### 8.6 Historical note on Radio Veritas Asia
RVA Vietnamese aired on shortwave to Vietnam for 49 years (from 1969; Vietnamese section among founding services) until shortwave ended **7/1/2018**, transitioning fully online. Decades of program formats (Lẽ Sống, Nói Với Giới Trẻ, Phút Cầu Nguyện, Kinh Mân Côi…) continue in the online grid and YouTube archive (§3.2, §7.3).
